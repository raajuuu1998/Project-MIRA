About MIRA

Project MIRA focuses on learning clinically meaningful signals from histopathology, including histopathology biomarkers, molecular biomarkers, molecular phenotypes, spatial immune phenotypes, and stromal architecture. The goal is to understand how these tissue-level patterns can support biomarker prediction and biological interpretation across cancers.

The project explores pathology foundation models, explainability, domain adaptation, cross-site and cross-cancer generalization, and transferable representations. A central aim is to develop simpler and more interpretable methods that remain robust across cohorts while reducing unnecessary model complexity.

Goals of the research project

  1. Develop biologically meaningful and transferable tissue representations for clinically relevant biomarker prediction.
  2. Study foundation models, explainability, and domain adaptation for robust generalization across cohorts, sites, and cancer types.
  3. Design simpler and more interpretable computational pathology methods that remain effective while reducing unnecessary model complexity.
